Free electrician exam prep
Flashcards and practice questions on the 2023 NEC, grounding and bonding, Wisconsin code changes and electrical safety. Free, no account needed.
Study practice written by VoltCE. These are not official exam questions and do not earn continuing-education credit. Always check the code book and your state licensing board for what applies to you.
Load calc & sizing practice
Thousands of free practice problems: conductor sizing, branch circuits, dwelling and commercial loads, motors, transformers with secondary protection, parallel conductors, conduit fill (several conduit types and insulations), voltage drop, grounding and box fill. Every answer comes with the worked solution.
Based on the 2023 NEC. Unless a problem says otherwise, assume 75°C-rated terminations, copper conductors and standard overcurrent device sizes from 240.6(A). Study practice written by VoltCE, not official exam questions, and no CE credit. Have your code book handy.
Code article study

Code book index lookup drill

States adopt different editions. Pick the edition you know and the edition you are studying, then review what changed, article by article, as flashcards or a quiz. Steps are chained: 2017 to 2023 shows the 2017-to-2020 and the 2020-to-2023 changes.
